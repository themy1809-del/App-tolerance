General items

Make sure to observe the following items to assure safe operation.

  1. Read the instruction manual.

  • Incorrect operating procedures can be extremely dangerous, and cause a fatal accident involving loss of life or accident resulting in injury or death.

Make sure to fully understand this manual before operating the boarding bridge.

  • Always store this instruction manual in a specified place so that every person handling the boarding bridge can read it repeatedly and thoroughly understand the boarding bridge.

  1. Wear safe clothes!

    • Wear neat clothes appropriate to actions.

Make sure to wear protective devices suitable for operation.

  1. Never operate the boarding bridge while you are in poor physical conditions or under the influence of alcohol or medicine.

    • You may be slow in judgment or give wrong judgment while you are in poor physical condition. Never operate the boarding bridge in such condition.

Never operate the boarding bridge while you are under the influence of alcohol or medicine or using an audio player or mobile phone.

  1. Inspect the boarding bridge.

  • If you find any abnormality, immediately contact a maintenance company.

    • Move the boarding bridge to the standby place or a place where the boarding bridge does not hinder operations of aircraft.

  • Inspection and maintenance

    • Perform “daily care” and “daily inspection and maintenance” by yourself as instructed in this manual. Store records of “daily care” and “daily inspection and maintenance” that will be important information in “specialized periodical inspection and maintenance”.

    • “Specialized periodical inspection and maintenance” require specialized knowledge.

Ask our company or a specialized maintenance company. Because “specialized periodical inspection and maintenance” require some time, it is recommended to stop spot operations.

  1. In case of an action against accident or fire

    • The customer is to determine actions to be taken against an accident and fire.

    • When a property damage accident occurs, immediately stop operating the boarding bridge, report the accident situation to our company, and follow our instructions.

    • When an accident resulting in injury or death occurs, immediately stop operating the boarding bridge, and give proper treatment to the injured.

    • When fire disaster occurs, immediately stop operating the boarding bridge, and evacuate people from the boarding bridge to a safe place.

    • Determine evacuation sites and locations of fire extinguisher to assure safety. It is recommended to periodically hold an evacuation drill.

  2. Action against fire disaster caused by earthquake, lighting stroke, typhoon, etc.

    • When an earthquake occurs, immediately stop operating the boarding bridge. When the earthquake intensity is 4 or more (Japanese scale), check for abnormalities.

    • When the boarding bridge is hit by a lightning stroke, immediately stop operating the boarding bridge, and check for abnormalities caused by the lightning stroke.

    • In the case of typhoon, the operating procedures are regulated by the wind velocity. Refer to the specification list.

(Refer to “Manual release of FB brake” in “3 PHASE MOTOR BRAKE” in “SECTION 11: Vendor Operation & Maintenance Manual” on page 16.)

Structure of Each Part

This section explains the structure of each part of the boarding bridge.

Rotunda … Refer to Fig. 2-13.

The rotunda connects the terminal building to the tunnel section A, and the boarding bridge turns around the rotunda during operation.

The floor, ceiling and corridor remain stationary even during operation. The rigid frame and roof turn around the rotunda column.

With regard to the left and right rotunda curtains, one is wound by the barrel and the other is unwound from the barrel as the boarding bridge turns.

The curtain always holds proper tension due to springs incorporated in the barrel.

The curtain guide roller and vertical roller work as the guides, and let the curtain fit along the ceiling and floor.

Rotunda column … Refer to Fig. 2-13.

The rotunda column supports the rotunda and works as the rotation axis while the rotunda turns during operation.

Tunnel … Refer to Figs. 2-14-1 and 2-14-2.

In the 2-section telescoping type, the tunnel consists of the tunnel sections A and B (from the rotunda side). In the 3-section telescoping type, the tunnel consists of the tunnel sections A, B and C (from the rotunda side).

The boarding bridge extends and retracts among the tunnel sections A, B and C. Several types of rollers are provided among the extending/retracting tunnel sections.

The tunnel is made of corrugated steel plates and steel plates. In the glass type, the tunnel side is made of glass.

  1. Rails and rollers … Figs. 2-14-1 and 2-14-2

Rollers are provided among the extending/retracting tunnel sections so that the boarding bridge smoothly extends and retracts. The upper portion of the tunnel works as a rail for roller movement.

The cable carrier is provided on the top face of the tunnel so that the cables for power, illumination, control and communication can follow up movements of the boarding bridge.

Wheel carriage … Refer to Fig. 2-17.

The wheel carriage consists of a frame, wheels, tires, chain drive mechanism and geared motor.

The frame of steel welding structure has two wheels.

A set of chain sprocket on the geared motor shaft side, chain sprocket on the wheel side and roller chain is provided on each of the left and right sides. The chain cover is provided to assure safety.

  1. Geared motor

The motor and gear box are integrated into the chain drive mechanism.

Refer to the separate sheet “Boarding Bridge Specification List” for the geared motor specification.

  1. Safety Hoop

The safety hoop is attached to the wheel carriage to prevent obstacles such as people from getting caught in the tires. The tape switch is attached to the tip of the safety hoop (only in the front and reverse directions of the tire) so that the boarding bridge will stop when an obstacle such as a person is detected.

  1. Outrigger (option)

The outrigger is provided on the both ends of the cross beam to prevent toppling of the boarding bridge caused by typhoon.

Use the outrigger when typhoon or strong wind is expected.

Drive column … Refer to Figs. 2-17 and 2-18.

The drive column raises and lowers the tunnel and cab. It is connected to the wheel carriage with trunnion pins, and consists of a swivel assembly, cross beam, a set of column assembly on the left and right sides and drive unit.

The vehicle direction detector and height detector are provided. The independent drive unit is provided at the end of the left and right columns respectively. The left and right columns move up and down by the vertical drive motor.

  1. Column assembly … Refer to Fig. 2-18.

The column assembly consists of a top section and bottom section made of square steel pipe and ball screws, and slides between the top section and the bottom section. The top section moves up and down by rotation of the ball screw.

  1. Ball screw assembly … Refer to Fig. 2-18.

The ball screw assembly consists of a set of ball screw, ball nut, beating mount, thrust bearing, etc. on each of the left and right sides. The upper end of the ball screw is directly connected to the vertical drive motor by way of the chain coupling. The ball nut is connected to the joint block at the upper end of the bottom section.

When each ball screw is rotated by the left and right vertical drive motors, the ball nut travels in the screw thread direction. By this mechanism, rotation of the screw is converted into linear motion of the nut.

  1. Drive unit

The drive unit consists of a vertical drive motor, top frame supporting the motor, frame, sprockets and height detector.

  1. Vertical drive motor … Refer to Fig. 2-18.

The boarding bridge is raised and lowered when the rotation force of the vertical drive motor is transmitted to the ball screws.

Refer to the separate sheet “Boarding Bridge Specification List” for the vertical drive motor specification.

  1. Upper and lower limit switches

The upper and lower limit switches provided in the left and right top sections and the striker provided in the bottom section adjust the upper and lower limits of the drive column.

  1. Wheel direction detector … Refer to Fig. 2-17.

To detect the wheel direction, the rotary encoder is provided in the center of the drive column cross beam, and directly connected to the wheel direction detection shaft provided at the upper end of the swivel assembly.

The rotation quantity of the wheel is transmitted as rotation of the detection shaft to the rotary encoder and displayed on the control console. The displayed value indicates the wheel direction against the tunnel.

The wheel rotation angle is limited to 87.5º maximum on each of the left and right sides by the rotary encoder.

  1. Height detector

The height detector is provided inside the top frame at the upper end of the top section on the left and right sides and counts the rotations of the vertical drive motor output shaft on the left and right sides.

The cab is located at the tip of the boarding bridge, is connected to an aircraft, and accommodates the operator’s seat for the boarding bridge.

The closure curtain seals an aircraft against the weather while the boarding bridge is connected to the aircraft.

  1. Auto leveler … Refer to Fig. 2-20.

Set the control key switch to “AUTO LEVEL” on the control console to push out the auto leveler arm toward an aircraft by the auto leveler drive motor.

The auto leveler arm is continuously pushed even after the wheel at the plunger tip touches the aircraft. After the spring built in the auto leveler arm applies proper pressure, rotation of the drive motor is stopped by the touch detection limit switch.

The wheel is continuously in contact with the aircraft body with proper pressure. The wheel switch detects vertical movements of the aircraft body, and lets the boarding bridge automatically follow up vertical movements of the aircraft body.

Accordingly, the relationship between the boarding bridge floor height and the aircraft floor height is maintained in the initial status.

When the clearance between the aircraft and the boarding bridge changes and the wheel pressing force becomes weaker while the auto leveler is operating, the limit switch is actuated to rotate the drive motor automatically, and the arm is pushed until proper pressing force is achieved. On the contrary, when the pressing force becomes stronger, the arm is pulled back until proper pressing force is achieved.

When the boarding bridge cannot follow up the aircraft due to abnormal changes in the aircraft floor height or for another reason, auto leveler warning appears on the control console and warning horn is issued to notify the operator of abnormality.

Turn the control key switch to “OFF” on the control console to accommodate the auto leveler arm by the auto leveler drive motor.

Curtain Adjustment

Adjustment of Cab Curtain

To adjust the cab curtain tension, proceed with the following steps:

  1. Rotate the cab fully in the right-side direction to adjust the right-hand curtain, and opposite for the left hand contain.

  2. Open the top cover of the curtain barrels.

  3. On the top of the curtain barrels is a spring tensioned. Using a wrench, maintain the spring tension and remove the locking pin.

  1. Turn the shaft counter-clockwise to increase the tension and clockwise to decrease the tension on the right hand barrel, and opposite for the left hand barrel.

  2. Replace the locking pin and rotate the cab in both directions.

  3. If the curtains wind and unwind smoothly, maintaining the required tension, close the top covers.

Adjustment of Rotunda Curtain

To adjust the rotunda curtain tension, proceed with the following steps:

  1. Rotate the “PAXWAY” fully in the right side direction to adjust the right hand curtain, and opposite for the left hand curtain.

  2. Open the hinged cover, which houses the two barrels.

  3. On the top of the curtain barrels is a spring tensioned. Using a wrench, maintain a spring tension and remove the locking pin.

  4. Turn the shaft clockwise to increase the tension and counter-clockwise to decrease the tension on the right hand barrel, and opposite for the left hand barrel.

  5. Replace the locking pin and rotate the rotunda in both directions.

  6. If the curtains wind and unwind smoothly, maintaining the required tension, close the hinged curtain covers.

Tunnel Roller Adjustment

Tunnel A lower front roller

Adjustment of this roller is not necessary because this roller is a datum point for adjusting the other rollers.

  1. Tunnel B lower rear roller

    1. Fully retract the PBB.

    2. Remove tunnel bottom and side access covers at locations of the rollers as well as weathering.

    3. After loosening nuts, adjust vertical adjusting bolts so that the clearances between tunnel A and B floors at the front and rear ends of the tunnels become equal.

    4. Adjust horizontal adjusting bolts so that left and right clearance between tunnel A and B side walls become equal. Loosen then the bolts by 1/2 turn to give the rollers smooth Contact with rails.

    5. After completing the adjustment, tighten the nuts firmly.

    6. Attach tunnel bottom and side access covers and weathering.

  2. Tunnel B upper front roller

    1. Fully retract the PBB.

    2. Remove tunnel roof and side access covers at locations of the rollers.

    3. After loosening nuts, adjust vertical and horizontal adjusting bolts so that the bolts are pressed to the rails and then loosen the bolts by 1/2 turn to give them smooth contact with the rails.

    4. After completing the adjustment, tighten the nuts firmly.

    5. Attach tunnel roof and side accesses covers.

  3. Tunnel B upper rear roller

Remove access covers and weathering and then follow the same procedures of (3) above.

  1. Tunnel A side front roller

    1. Fully extend the PBB.

    2. Open tunnel floor ramp.

    3. After loosening nuts, adjust bolts so that left and right tunnel B lower front rollers are pressed to rails equally. Loosen then the bolts by 1/2 turn to give the rollers smooth contact with rails.

    4. After completing the adjustment, tighten the double nuts firmly.

  2. Tunnel alignment roller

    1. Loosen double nuts of roller shaft.

    2. Adjust roller shaft so that the rollers are pressed to tunnel rails.

    3. Loosen the bolts by 1/2 turn and then tighten the double nuts firmly.
